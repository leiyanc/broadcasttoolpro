## English

### 1. Who is responsible

Orion Media LLC, a Florida, United States company, operates Broadcast Tool Pro.

- Privacy and security contact: `security@broadcasttoolpro.com`
- Business mailing address: 18331 Pines Blvd, Unit #153, Pembroke Pines,
  FL 33029, United States

For account, subscription, security, and support information, Orion Media LLC
acts as the data controller. When a customer uploads operational content and
determines why it is processed, Orion Media LLC generally processes that
content on the customer's instructions as a service provider or processor.

### 2. Information and purposes

We process account and organization details, authentication and security
events, subscription and billing references, support and privacy requests,
basic service logs, customer-uploaded operational files, and generated output.
We use this information to provide the service, administer accounts and
subscriptions, secure and troubleshoot the platform, communicate about the
service, meet legal obligations, and establish or defend legal claims.

Depending on the context, the legal basis may be performance of a contract,
steps requested before entering a contract, compliance with a legal
obligation, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the service, or
consent when specifically requested. We do not sell personal information.

### 3. Providers and international transfers

We use a limited set of service providers: Render for application
infrastructure, Amazon Web Services for transactional email, Google for
authorized communications and encrypted off-site backups, and Stripe for
subscriptions and payments. Broadcast Tool Pro does not store full payment
card numbers.

Information may be processed in the United States or another country where a
provider operates. Before this notice is published for European customers, we
will verify and document the applicable provider agreements and transfer
safeguards, such as an adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses.

### 4. Retention

- Temporary uploads and generated working files: 24 hours.
- Eligible account records after account closure: delete or de-identify within
  30 days.
- Generated and archived operational reports after account closure: 90 days.
- Support, privacy-request, authentication, and security audit history: 24
  months after closure or case resolution, whichever is later.
- Local backups: 14 days. Encrypted remote backups: seven daily and four weekly
  recovery points.
- Billing, tax, dispute, and legally required records: the applicable legal or
  contractual period.

A legal hold, security incident, dispute, or statutory duty may require a
documented exception. Residual backup copies expire through normal rotation.

### 5. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, a person may request access, correction, deletion,
restriction, portability, or objection; may withdraw consent when processing
depends on consent; and may complain to the competent data protection
authority. Requests may be submitted in the authenticated Help Center or to
`security@broadcasttoolpro.com`. We verify identity and authority before
disclosing or deleting organization data and normally respond within one
month, subject to lawful extensions.

We do not use customer information for solely automated decisions that produce
legal or similarly significant effects.

### 2. Información y finalidades

Procesamos datos de cuenta y organización, eventos de autenticación y
seguridad, referencias de suscripción y facturación, solicitudes de soporte y
privacidad, registros técnicos básicos, archivos operacionales cargados por el

Según el contexto, la base jurídica puede ser la ejecución de un contrato,
medidas solicitadas antes de contratar, una obligación legal, el interés
legítimo de operar y proteger el servicio, o el consentimiento cuando se

### 3. Proveedores y transferencias internacionales

Usamos un grupo limitado de proveedores: Render para infraestructura, Amazon
Web Services para correo transaccional, Google para comunicaciones autorizadas

La información puede procesarse en Estados Unidos u otro país donde opere un

una decisión de adecuación o Cláusulas Contractuales Tipo.

### 4. Retención

- Cargas temporales y archivos de trabajo generados: 24 horas.
- Datos de cuenta elegibles después del cierre: eliminar o desidentificar en
  un plazo de 30 días.
- Informes operacionales generados y archivados después del cierre: 90 días.

- Copias locales: 14 días. Copias externas cifradas: siete puntos diarios y
  cuatro semanales.
- Registros de facturación, impuestos, disputas y exigidos legalmente: el plazo
  legal o contractual aplicable.

Una obligación legal, litigio, incidente de seguridad o disputa puede exigir
una excepción documentada. Las copias residuales expiran mediante la rotación
normal de respaldos.

### 5. Sus derechos

Sujeto a la ley aplicable, una persona puede solicitar acceso, rectificación,
supresión, limitación, portabilidad u oposición; retirar el consentimiento
cuando esa sea la base del tratamiento; y reclamar ante la autoridad de
protección de datos competente. Las solicitudes pueden enviarse desde Help
Center autenticado o a `security@broadcasttoolpro.com`. Verificamos identidad y
autoridad antes de divulgar o eliminar datos de una organización y normalmente
respondemos en un mes, sujeto a prórrogas permitidas por la ley.

No usamos información del cliente para decisiones exclusivamente automatizadas
que produzcan efectos jurídicos o de importancia similar.
