Transactional Email Policy

Broadcast Tool Pro uses email for requested account and operational communications, with controls for delivery failures and complaints.

Effective July 30, 2026

1. Messages we send

Transactional messages may include account creation, access-request status, trial or subscription reminders, security notices, support updates, and operational service notifications. These messages are triggered by a user action, an account relationship, or a necessary service event.

2. Recipient source

Recipients provide their address directly, belong to an authorized customer organization, or are designated operational contacts. Broadcast Tool Pro does not use purchased, rented, or scraped email lists and does not permit customers to use the service for bulk unsolicited email.

3. Delivery controls

Email is queued and delivered through an approved provider. Delivery, bounce, complaint, and rejection events are recorded. Provider event notifications are authenticated before they can change delivery state.

4. Bounces and complaints

Addresses associated with permanent delivery failures or complaints are suppressed from future delivery. An administrator may remove a suppression only after verifying that the address is valid and the recipient expects the applicable transactional messages.

5. Recipient control

Recipients may report an unexpected message through the Help Center or Request Access channel. Essential account or security messages do not contain promotional list content. Marketing campaigns are outside the current purpose of this email system.

6. Monitoring

Platform administrators can review queue health, successful and failed deliveries, complaints, bounces, and suppressed recipients. These records support incident response and responsible sending.
