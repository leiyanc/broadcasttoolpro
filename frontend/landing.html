ABOUT US

Leiyan Cotayo

With more than 20 years of experience in television channel operations, Leiyan Cotayo has led the launch, management, distribution, and migration of channels across the United States and Latin America. His expertise spans content distribution, master control operations, platform migrations, and the delivery of reliable broadcast and digital viewing experiences.

Throughout his career, he has held operational roles at Telemundo, TeleFutura—now UniMás—Mega TV, and Mediapro US. He has contributed to the launch and expansion of major television networks and services, including beIN Sports in English and Spanish, DIRECTV Sports+, HOLA TV for the U.S. and Latin America, Centroamérica TV, TV Dominicana, and Pasiones for the U.S. and Latin America.

His experience also includes the development of TARIMA TV and COMERCIO TV in collaboration with his current business partners. These digital and linear channels were created to elevate Hispanic culture and business through premium content and innovation. Building and operating them gave him firsthand insight into the recurring challenges behind programming data, XMLTV delivery, traffic workflows, platform requirements, and operational reporting. That experience directly shaped Broadcast Tool Pro’s focus on practical, reliable tools for the people responsible for keeping channels running.
